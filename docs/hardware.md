# Hardware

What the plugin does per UGREEN model. The tables between the markers are generated from `web/models/` by `docs/tools/gen-docs.ts` (`just docs`); edit the model files, not the tables.

## How verification works

Every model has four capabilities that are checked on their own: **LEDs**, **Fan control**, **Case view** and **Sensors** (temperatures and the dashboard tile). Each one has a record in `web/models/<id>.ts` under `verified`:

| Field     | Meaning                                                            |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `status`  | `verified`, `community`, `partial`, `expected` or `unsupported`    |
| `by`      | Who checked: `maintainer (tower)`, `miskcoo`, `users`              |
| `version` | The plugin release it was checked on (a heading of `CHANGELOG.md`) |
| `date`    | `YYYY-MM-DD`                                                       |
| `ref`     | Issue, PR or forum post with the evidence                          |
| `note`    | What exactly was seen, or what is still open                       |

| State       | Meaning                                                                                 | Needs                                 |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
| Verified    | A maintainer ran it on their own device with the plugin.                                | `by`, and `version` + `date` or `ref` |
| Community   | Users, or miskcoo's driver for the LED protocol, confirmed it on the model.             | `by` or `ref`                         |
| Partial     | Checked on a device, but not everything works or is confirmed.                          | `by` and a `note` saying what is open |
| Expected    | The code covers the model; nobody has confirmed it.                                     | Nothing; no date                      |
| Not offered | The plugin does not offer it on this model (no fan wiring known, no case drawing, ...). | Nothing                               |

The overall state of a model is derived, never written down: **Verified** when every capability the plugin offers is verified, **Partly verified** when at least one is, **Community-tested** when none is but users confirmed one, otherwise **Expected**.

Maintainer boxes: the DXP4800 GT (the "tower") and the DXP6800 Pro (the upstream development box). Checks older than three releases are listed as stale by `just models-check` and are re-tested before a release, see [releasing.md](releasing.md#re-test-the-maintainer-boxes). A report from a user (`ugreen-nas-ctl report`, [cli.md](cli.md#report-a-model)) turns an _expected_ capability into _community_; a maintainer test turns it into _verified_.

Models that cannot run Unraid (ARM): [faq.md](faq.md#which-models-cannot-run-it).

- **DX4600 Pro**: First-generation four-bay model, no longer listed on the UGREEN store. No case view or fan control.
- **DX4700+**: Four bays, no longer listed on the UGREEN store; the board reports its name as DX4700+. No case view or fan control.
- **DXP480T Plus**: All-flash model: no SATA bays, four M.2 slots, one 10G LAN port. Only the power LED works.
- **DXP2800**: Two bays, two M.2 slots, one 2.5G LAN port. The V1.2 board (December 2025) has a new LED chip: the plugin falls back to the frame UGREEN ships for it, waiting for a report from a V1.2 owner.
- **DXP2800 GT**: Two bays (U.2 capable), two M.2 slots, one 10G LAN port. LEDs are enabled but nobody has confirmed them. Case view (front, rear) after the product photos; no fan control (Chipsea CS201x embedded controller, read-only probe: ugreen-nas-fan cs201x).
- **DXP4800**: Four bays, two M.2 slots, one 2.5G LAN port. Fan control after a fan probe.
- **DXP4800 GT**: Four bays (SATA or U.2), two M.2 slots, two 10G LAN ports. Fan control through the IT5571 embedded controller; LEDs through /dev/mem.
- **DXP4800 Plus / Pro**: Four bays, two M.2 slots, one 10G and one 2.5G LAN port. Fan control after a fan probe.
- **DXP4800S**: Four bays, two M.2 slots. Sources disagree on the LAN ports (one or two 2.5G). Nobody has reported this model yet.
- **DXP6800 Pro**: Six bays, two M.2 slots plus the internal system slot, a PCIe slot, two 10G LAN ports. Fan control through the IT8613E; the reference model of the plugin.
- **DXP6800 Ultra**: Six bays, two M.2 slots, two 10G LAN ports and two Thunderbolt 4 ports. LEDs are enabled but nobody has confirmed them.
- **DXP8800 Plus**: Eight bays, two M.2 slots, two 10G LAN ports and two Thunderbolt 4 ports. Fan control after a fan probe.
- **DXP8800 Ultra**: Eight bays, two M.2 slots, two 10G LAN ports and two Thunderbolt 4 ports. LEDs are enabled but nobody has confirmed them.
- **iDX6011 / iDX6012 / Pro**: Six bays, two M.2 slots, a display at the front (Pro: 3.7 inch LCD), two 10G LAN ports, two Thunderbolt 4 ports, OCuLink. Fan control on the Pro only; the plain iDX6011 has none.

## Add a model

See [development.md](development.md#add-a-model). A new model starts with `expected` for every capability the plugin offers; `just models-check` rejects a record without the fields its state needs.

Case drawings: the DXP4800 family, DXP2800 and iDX6011 / Pro are drawn after product photos, the DXP8800 Plus as a row of ports after review port lists. M.2 slots are mapped for the DXP6800 Pro and DXP4800 GT only.

Fan wiring probe for models with unknown headers: [fan.md](fan.md#fan-probe-for-unknown-wiring). Related projects: [competition.md](competition.md).
